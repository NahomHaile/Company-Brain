# Thicket — August 2026

Hi all — August was the month we finally fixed onboarding, and it cost us most of the month to do it.

## Metrics

- MRR: $37,800 (up from $35,100 in July)
- Net new logos: 5, churned 1
- Cash: $1.17M, burn ~$74K/month

## Highlights

We rebuilt onboarding end to end. The old flow assumed one location and one calendar, which is not how any of our better customers actually work. Early signal is good — the three practices that came through the new flow were live in under two weeks, against a median of about a month before.

Two multi-site conversations opened in the Hudson Valley. Both came from existing customers, which is the first time referrals have driven our pipeline rather than cold outbound.

## Lowlights

We lost a single-doctor practice in July that I did not see coming. They were quiet for six weeks and then cancelled. I do not have a clean read on why, and that bothers me more than the revenue — $290/month is noise, not knowing is not.

Sales cycles on multi-location deals are running 6-8 weeks, roughly double what I modelled. That changes the runway math if it holds.

## Asks

If you know practice managers at groups running three or more locations in the Northeast, I would like an introduction. That is the profile where we win.

Still looking for a part-time implementation person — someone who has worked in a clinic, not a support hire.

— Maya
